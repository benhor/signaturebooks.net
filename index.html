Horatio M. Bennett

Honorary Doctor of Philosphy

(Honoris Causa)

Dr. Horatio M. Bennett, a native of Jamaica and longtime U.S. resident, has a certificate in Positive Psychology from Harvard Medical School, and attained his Honorary Ph.D. from American Management University. He is listed in the Detroit chapter of Who’s Who in Black America (2020) and a nominee, Michigan’s Men of Excellence, 2025. The author of ten books, he also serves as director of the Horatio and Natasha Bennett Foundation, and LegacyBridge, both nonprofit entities dedicated to community upliftment!

“The written word is rebellion made permanent. Spoken oracles blaze briefly, dazzling crowds, then vanish
into the frailty of memory. Writing is the sorcery that refuses to die. Ink is a defiant spirit, unbothered by
priests, prophets, or the trembling theatrics of revelation. Oracles demand belief; writing demands reckoning.
The written word stalks centuries, confronting each generation with the same unblinking truth. It is the fire that
does not wait for permission, the shadow that outlives its caster. Speech evaporates. Writing haunts. And in
that haunting, it becomes the true oracle; one no human voice can silence.”

From the Mind of Horatio
